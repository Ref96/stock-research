# Palmistry Skill

**Sources**: Cheiro *Palmistry for All* (1916) — primary system; D'Arpentigny *La Chirognomonie* (1843) — hand classification; Nathaniel Altman *Sexual Palmistry* (1986) — psychological and relational dimensions; practitioner notes from a witch family lineage (手相笔记.docx, 女巫掌.docx).

**Language rule**: Detect the user's language. Answer in the same language throughout the reading.

## Section 0 — Reader Ethics (skip with "skip ethics")

These are the house rules of a witch family lineage. Present them briefly if the user is new to palmistry readings, or if they ask "how does this work". The user can say "skip ethics" or "skip the rules" to go straight to the reading.

1. **Consent only** — only read for someone who has asked. Never volunteer a reading for a third party.
2. **No reading close intimates** — too much prior knowledge creates bias; hard to separate intuition from assumption.
3. **Strict confidentiality** — what comes up in a reading stays between reader and subject. No sharing with third parties except where harm is imminent.
4. **Unfavourable signs → constructive framing** — don't predict doom. Reframe as: "This pattern suggests you may face X — here is how to approach it." Never predict death.
5. **Never predict death** — this is an absolute rule with both practical and metaphysical reasons.
6. **Energy exchange** — a reading should not be entirely free. Even a coin, a song, or a meal counts.

## Section 1 — Reading Protocol

Always follow this sequence. **Do not skip steps.**

```
1. Which hand (dominant vs non-dominant)
2. Hand shape → Seven-type (D'Arpentigny/Cheiro) + Four-element cross-check
3. Hand quality → consistency, flexibility, skin texture, temperature (Altman)
4. Thumb + four fingers
5. Mounts
6. Major lines (Head LINE FIRST — it is the compass)
   → Head → Life → Heart → Fate → Sun
7. Minor lines and special marks
8. Psychic / Witch Markers (if asked)
9. Sexual & Relational (if asked) → load references/sexual.md
10. Compatibility (if two people's hands) → load references/sexual.md
11. Synthesis — always end with an integrated picture, not a list of disconnected facts
```

**Dominant vs Non-dominant hand**:
- Non-dominant = inherited potential, early self, past
- Dominant = current expression, what you are doing with what you were given
- When the two hands differ significantly, a major life transformation has occurred or is underway
- Always ask which hand is dominant if not obvious

## Section 2 — Hand Shape

### A. Seven-Type System (D'Arpentigny + Cheiro)

| Type | Visual markers | Core character |
|---|---|---|
| **Elementary** | Short, thick, blunt palm; stubby fingers | Instinct-driven, sensory-first, low intellectual complexity |
| **Square** | Palm roughly square; square fingertips | Practical, orderly, reliable; reason dominates feeling |
| **Spatulate** | Fingertips fan out like a palette knife | High energy, action-driven, adventurous, impulsive |
| **Philosophic** | Long fingers with prominent knuckles | Analytical, contemplative, sometimes over-theoretical |
| **Conic/Artistic** | Palm slightly narrowed; softly tapered tips | Sensory, impulsive, aesthetically sharp, emotionally volatile |
| **Psychic** | Very long, slender, sharply pointed tips | Deeply idealistic, fragile, often disconnected from practical reality |
| **Mixed** | Characteristics of two or more types | Versatile but potentially directionless — identify the dominant type |

**Practical note**: Most people are Mixed. Find the dominant type and use secondary features as modifiers.

### B. Four-Element System (modern synthesis)

Evaluate all four indicators before deciding. 2-of-4 is the minimum for a call; 3-of-4 is confident.

| Element | Palm shape | Fingers vs palm | Fingertip form | Line density |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Earth** | Square (wide) | Fingers shorter than palm | Square, broad | Few but deep |
| **Fire** | Rectangle (long) | Fingers ≤ palm length | Spatulate/wide | Many; a few very deep main lines |
| **Water** | Rectangle (long) | Fingers ≥ palm length | Narrow, tapered | Dense, fine, networked |
| **Air** | Square (wide) | Fingers longer than palm | Conic or knotted | Clear, moderate density |

**Key disambiguation rule**: Water and Fire both have long palms — distinguish by **fingertip shape** (Water = tapered; Fire = spatulate/wide) and **line quality** (Water = fine dense web; Fire = heavy dominant lines). When indicators tie 2:2, fingertip and line quality take precedence over palm shape and finger ratio.

**Finger length measurement**: Measure from the finger's basal crease (the actual bone joint), not the webbing. Webbing creates a visual bias that makes fingers look shorter than they are.

**Element core profiles**:

| Element | Core drive | Strengths | Pitfalls |
|---|---|---|---|
| Earth | Security & stability | Reliable, grounded, enduring | Rigid, unimaginative, change-averse |
| Fire | Passion & action | Drive, charisma, leadership | Scattered, impulsive, burns out |
| Water | Feeling & intuition | Empathy, depth, creativity | Boundary dissolution, low agency |
| Air | Thought & communication | Logic, articulation, adaptability | Emotional distance, over-intellectualisation |

### C. Hand Type vs Line Conflict

**Principle**: Hand type = the energy available; lines = how that energy is actually being used. Conflict = internal tension.

Common conflict patterns:

- **Fire hand + joined head/life line at origin**: Naturally driven and passionate, but the braking system is too strong. The tension is "I want to act boldly but caution/sensitivity holds me back." More painful than a Water type, who accepts their quietness; this person knows they should be fierce and can't get there. Source of deep internal friction.
- **Water hand + strong fate line**: Highly receptive nature, easily carried into trajectories chosen by others rather than self.
- **Earth hand + deeply descending head line**: Practically-presented exterior concealing strong imaginative and creative needs; creativity chronically suppressed.
- **Air hand + deeply marked heart line**: Rational exterior with profound emotional needs that don't know how to surface.

> When shape and lines conflict: the hand shape shows **who you want to be**; the lines show **who you've become**. Larger divergence = deeper inner conflict.

## Section 3 — Hand Quality (Altman dimensions)

These four qualities modify everything. Always assess them before reading lines.

### Consistency (squeeze test)

Press the palm gently. Assess resistance:

| Consistency | Sexual/relational profile |
|---|---|
| **Flabby** (collapses easily) | Low physical energy and drive; more receiver than giver; may intellectualise feelings; emotionally 'flat' |
| **Soft** (yields without resistance) | Lower drive but warmer than flabby; when soft+thick → heightened sensuality, possible over-indulgence |
| **Elastic** (springs back) | Adaptable, vital, open to new experiences; healthy sexual energy |
| **Firm** (slight yield) | Physically active, psychologically stable, strong; may be stubborn; needs time to adapt |
| **Hard** (no give, coarse) | Abundant physical energy but psychologically rigid; 'heart' feelings blocked; energy can erupt as anger |

### Flexibility (bend test)

Hold the back of the hand and gently bend fingers back:

| Flexibility | Character |
|---|---|
| Very flexible (90°+) | Highly impressionable; generous to a fault; difficulty committing; unpredictable |
| Moderately flexible (graceful arc) | Versatile on emotional and sexual levels; more reliable; some tendency to spread desire across multiple interests |
| Firm (small bend) | Careful with feelings; reliable; needs to feel safe before opening up; rarely impulsive |
| Stiff (barely moves) | Psychologically rigid; stubborn; difficulty compromising; deep feelings present but hard to express; very good at keeping secrets |

### Skin Texture

- **Fine skin**: High physical and emotional sensitivity; easily affected by environment; requires harmony to function well
- **Coarse skin**: More resilient and grounded; less tuned to subtle emotional cues; partner may need to be more explicit

### Hand Temperature (quick assessment)

- **Warm**: Outgoing, warm-hearted, expressive
- **Cold**: Emotionally reserved or withholding; can indicate low circulation or chronic withdrawal

## Section 4 — Thumb and Fingers

### The Thumb (single most important individual feature)

The thumb is the "barometer of will." D'Arpentigny: "The thumb individualises the man."

**Length**:
- Long: Will governs action; clear purpose; ability to direct others
- Short: Brute force and stubbornness over reason; emotionally-driven action

**Tip shape**:
- Club-shaped/bulbous ("murderer's thumb"): Energy accumulates and erupts; unpredictable under stress — do not conclude without reading the whole hand

**First phalange joint**:
- Waisted (thin at joint): Diplomatic, intuitive, flexible thinker
- Thick/full: Direct to the point of bluntness

**Flexibility**:
- Bends back 90°+: Generous, easy-going, prone to over-promising; weaker will
- Moderate bend: Balanced reliability
- Barely bends: Stubborn, conservative, emotionally reserved but trustworthy

**Angle of opening**:
- Near 90° (low-set): Independent, confident, unconventional
- Close to palm (high-set): Emotionally suppressed; more closed character

**Thumb as sexual indicator (Altman)**:
- Flexible thumb = generous sexually; open to partners' needs; may be taken advantage of
- Stiff thumb = direct sexual style; lower adaptability; may withhold emotionally
- High-set thumb (45° to index finger) = Victorian sexual attitudes; overly cautious; embarrassed by sexuality
- Low-set, wide thumb = sexually integrated; adventurous; comfortable with body

### Four Fingers

| Finger | Planet | Core meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Index (Jupiter) | Jupiter | Ambition, leadership, self-regard; long = drive to command; bent = unscrupulous methods |
| Middle (Saturn) | Saturn | Responsibility, introspection, fate-consciousness; very long = melancholy; straight = steady |
| Ring (Apollo) | Sun | Creativity, desire for recognition; long + sun line = gambling streak |
| Little (Mercury) | Mercury | Communication, business instinct; long = eloquence; bent = persuasive but potentially manipulative |

**Knuckle prominence**:
- Smooth joints: Impulsive, intuitive, emotionally expressive
- Knotted joints (not arthritic): Analytical, careful, not easily deceived

**Finger spacing** (hand held open naturally):
- Wide spacing: Open, communicative, spontaneous
- Fingers held close together: Insecurity, guardedness, psychological closure

**Self-esteem indicators (Altman)**:
- Index = Apollo in length: Healthy self-esteem baseline
- Index shorter than Apollo: Tendency to underestimate oneself; seeks relationships to feel validated
- Index longer and strong: High self-regard; can become vanity if life/head lines are joined

**Jealousy indicator**: Index finger curving toward Saturn = innate jealousy and possessiveness. The greater the curve (excluding arthritis), the more pronounced.

## Section 5 — Mounts

Fleshy pads at the palm's base and finger roots. Full = energy present; flat = energy weak; excessive = energy runs to extremes.

| Mount | Location | Positive (full) | Negative (excessive / flat) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Jupiter** | Below index finger | Ambition, leadership, spiritual sense | Arrogance, domination / low confidence |
| **Saturn** | Below middle finger | Prudence, responsibility, science | Melancholy, isolation / recklessness |
| **Apollo** (Sun) | Below ring finger | Artistry, luck, recognition | Vanity, gambling / mediocrity |
| **Mercury** | Below little finger | Business, eloquence, science | Cunning / difficulty expressing |
| **Upper Mars** (active) | Between thumb and index | Initiative, courage | Aggression, violence |
| **Lower Mars** (passive) | Mid-palm, little-finger side | Endurance, moral courage | Cruelty / cowardice |
| **Luna** (Moon) | Lower palm, little-finger side | Imagination, intuition, travel | Excessive fantasy / dullness |
| **Venus** | Inside the thumb (within life line arc) | Love, passion, vitality | Excess / sensual coldness |

**Venus Mount detail (key sexual indicator)**:
- Full and elastic: Warmth, strong vitality, healthy sexual energy
- Full and soft: Desire present but low action; comfort-seeking; may remain in fantasy
- Full and hard: High energy but rigid expression; may lack tenderness in intimacy
- Flat: Low interest in physical intimacy; not necessarily cold-hearted but body not a priority
- Life line cutting through Venus (rather than arcing around it): Coldness; sexuality not a priority; prudish qualities

**Venus colour**: Reddish grille lines on the mount intensify physical passion and urgent desire for release.

## Section 6 — Major Lines

### Reading Principles (Cheiro, mandatory)

1. **Head line first** — it is the compass. Read direction of thinking before anything else.
2. Deep and clear > wide and vague. Deep = focused will; wide = physical energy but scattered intention.
3. **Chained** = weakness in that line's domain — instability, not strength.
4. **Island** = a period of difficulty or decline. Always negative wherever found.
5. **Star** on Sun mount/Sun line = brilliant luck. Elsewhere = crisis.
6. **Square** = protection. Reduces impact of negative marks nearby.

---

### 1. Head Line (智慧线 / 理智线) — READ FIRST

Starts near the life line origin, crosses the palm.

**Origin**:
- Joined to life line: High sensitivity; lacks self-confidence; overly cautious; deeply family-oriented; ideal team member but slow to act independently
- Inside life line: Nervousness, irritability, poor self-control
- Separate from life line: Self-confidence; fast judgment; independent action — the wider the gap, the more impulsive

**High vs low start**:
- High (closer to Jupiter finger): Naturally independent; strong learner; ambitious
- Low (below normal start): Low drive; passive; prone to escapism (gaming, fantasy)

**Trajectory and endpoint (most important)**:

| Path | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Straight across | Practical, commercial, logical; lacks imagination |
| Slight downward curve | Ideal balance — practical + imaginative |
| Deep descent into Luna | Strong imagination; emotionally-led decisions; artistic talent; potential melancholy; psychic sensitivity |
| Extreme descent (near wrist) | Emotional intensity bordering on self-destructive; needs full hand context |
| Upward toward Mercury | Growing fixation on money/material success with age |

**Length**:
- Long: Careful, deep thinker; considers all angles
- Short: Not lack of intelligence — single-track expert; specialist mentality; uninterested in tangential knowledge
- Long + straight = high logical intelligence but traditional; low creativity
- Long + descending = rich inner life; intuitive; imaginative; possible psychic sensitivity

**Line quality**:
- Deep: Excellent concentration; undistracted
- Fine: Open, generous, expressive
- Chained or faint: Mental instability; poor focus; risk of depression, addiction
- Wavy: Indecision; emotional swings; each curve correlates with a crisis point
- Broken + overlapping: Major cognitive restructuring at that age; life pivot

**Special forms**:
- Writer's Fork (Y-split at end): Dual thinking style — creative and practical simultaneously; strong writing and narrative talent
- Double head line (rare): Can sustain two completely different mental careers simultaneously

### 2. Life Line (生命线)

Arcs around the Venus mount from near the thumb web down toward the wrist.

**The life line measures quality and vitality of life — NOT lifespan.**

**Origin cluster**:
- Many small lines at start: Early-life instability; possible bullying or family disruption; "problem youth" pattern
- Starting from Jupiter mount (high): Driven by ambition; high self-discipline
- Starting from Mars area (low): Quick temper; combative

**Arc size**:
- Wide arc: Strong vitality; physical magnetism; large sphere of action
- Tight to thumb: More limited physical energy; not necessarily short life
- Arc wide in early section, tightens later: Expansive young adulthood; energy becomes more inward and selective with age — may be chosen, or may be caused by a specific life experience

**Contact with head line**:
- Doesn't touch head line: Very independent; some jealousy; self-centred streak; realistic
- Joins head line at start: Family-oriented; loyal; good emotional intelligence; cautious
- Parallel section with head line: Highly family-dependent; exceptionally reliable partner

**Line quality**:
- Deep + thick + simple: Enormous vitality; can be overbearing
- Deep + fine: Vital, open, generous
- Chained: Physical instability; digestive issues; socially active but emotionally inconsistent; wide network, shallow roots
- Short: Not early death. Indicates early psychological trauma that dampened life-engagement; a kind of "going through the motions" quality in life
- Island: Health or life difficulty during that period
- Break without overlap: Serious health crisis or life interruption
- Break with overlap: Forced life change — different path, not recovery of the old one

**Tail formations**:
- Forked tail toward Luna: Driven to leave hometown or native country; fated traveller
- Frayed tail (tassel): Mental scatter; difficulty completing things; poor sustained focus
- Two life lines: Can carry two simultaneous life tracks (not necessarily "shadow personality" as popularised)

**Support lines**:
- Mars line (inner life line parallel): Reserve of life force; absorbs damage from breaks and islands
- Upward branches from life line: Moments of ambition or advancement; direction = which mount = which domain

### 3. Heart Line (感情线)

From the little-finger side, running across the upper palm. **Represents emotional and psychological love — not physical desire alone.**

**Origin (most important)**:

| Starting point | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Outer edge of Jupiter mount | Love becomes blind worship; easily devastated; emotionally most vulnerable type |
| Centre of Jupiter mount | Ideal — loyal, high moral standard, devoted when committed |
| Between index and middle fingers | Deep, inward love; willing to sacrifice; does not demand perfection from partner |
| Below Saturn (middle finger) | Self-centred love; more taker than giver; low tolerance for others' failings |

**Length**:
- Very long (reaching across palm): Extreme jealousy
- Short: Somewhat self-interested; poor at relating to others' emotional reality; risk of addictive behaviour

**Quality**:
- Deep: Strong capacity for feeling
- Pale and wide: Emotional detachment; surface-level affection
- Chained: Emotionally inconsistent; difficult to sustain deep attachment; socially active in love, rarely deeply settled
- Straight: Feeling over reason; low analytical capacity in relationships
- Wavy: Emotionally fickle; sees multiple people simultaneously or in rapid sequence

**Terminal direction**:
- Curves down toward head/life line junction: Realist; makes emotional decisions based on logic and self-interest alone
- Curves upward to Jupiter mount bottom: Feeling constantly overrules judgment; "heart over head" in its most total form

**Simian Line** (heart and head lines merge into one horizontal line):
- About 1–5% of population; slightly more common in men
- Extreme stubbornness and intensity; either/or thinking; difficulty separating emotion from reason
- Higher association with addictive behaviours (alcohol, obsessive patterns)
- Can coexist with exceptional focus and achievement when energy is well-directed

**Forks on the heart line**:
- Downward fork at midpoint (toward head line): An emotional wound severe enough to alter judgment and life decisions — not ordinary heartbreak; value-system-level disruption
- Upward fork near endpoint (toward Jupiter): After damage, ideals become higher not lower. "If I've suffered this much, the next must be perfect." Sets bar so high most candidates are eliminated. Combined with the midpoint fork = wounded and then walled off
- Fishtail split at end: Permanent inner split between two emotional needs; chronic dissatisfaction
- Three-pronged end: Great capacity for love; inclusive, self-sacrificial; deep loyalty

**Two heart lines**: Enormous emotional depth; very high investment in family and raising children.

### 4. Fate Line (命运线 / 土星线)

Runs upward, generally from wrist area toward the middle finger base. **Represents external shaping forces on life, not success or failure per se.**

**Absence of fate line**:
- Positive reading: Self-determined; doesn't believe in fate; relies entirely on personal will — check head line quality
- Negative reading: Drifts without direction; lacks long-term planning; potential for chronic avoidance behaviour

**Origin**:

| Starting point | Meaning |
|---|---|
| From wrist straight up | Born fortunate; clear life direction (needs sun line to confirm) |
| Inside the life line (near Venus) | Early life shaped/constrained by family; may inherit family business; breakthrough from this constraint is the defining life moment |
| From Luna mount | Destiny linked to others' whims and imagination; romantic but variable; career dependent on public goodwill |
| Appears mid-palm only | Early years difficult; own path emerges only in mid-life |

**Line quality**:
- Deep and long: Strong sense of purpose; suited to large organisations or government
- Two fate lines simultaneously: Natural "slash" career person — manages two distinct occupational tracks

**Intersections**:
- Cut by head line: Own misjudgment ends the opportunity (approx. age 35)
- Cut by heart line: Romantic disruption terminates career trajectory (approx. age 45–50)
- Extending toward Jupiter: Power, leadership — excellent outcome

**Breaks and shifts**:
- Break with restart at offset position: Not recovery — an entirely different road was taken
- Break + island/chain on life line at same time: Compounded crisis; multiple simultaneous hits

### 5. Sun Line / Success Line (太阳线 / 成功线)

Runs upward to the Apollo mount (below ring finger). The fate line maps effort; the sun line maps recognition and reward.

**Without a sun line**: Talent exists but struggles to be seen. Even successes may feel hollow internally — "so what?" after achieving.

| Sun line present + fate line present | Effort + recognition — the ideal combination |
|---|---|
| Sun line without fate line | Lucky type; doesn't need conventional career arc to prosper; tends to be well-provided for in later life through fortune and others' goodwill |
| Fate line without sun line | Diligent but invisible; earns through effort without recognition or windfall |

**Origin**:
- From life line: Success earned through personal discipline and hard work
- From Luna: Success through public affection (performers, artists, influencers)
- From head line: Recognition through expertise; typically manifests after 30–40
- From heart line: Achievement through relationship or emotional harmony; late flourishing

**Line quality**:
- Deep + straight + long: Exceptional — early recognition, sustained success
- Short but clear: Strong reputation in a specific domain even without broad fame
- Multiple parallel lines: Multi-talented; multiple income streams; risk of energy dispersion
- Wavy or broken: Uneven progress; inconsistent self-belief
- Island: A period of damaged reputation or public difficulty
- Star: Sudden, spectacular luck or recognition
- Cross: Damaged reputation; financial loss from poor decision

## Section 8 — Witch Markers / Psychic Markers

> From European witch tradition and corresponding cross-cultural equivalents (Cantonese 仙骨, Taiwanese 帶天命, shamanic traditions worldwide). These markers indicate raw material — whether it is developed is another matter. Check the **dominant hand** only. No male-left / female-right distinction.

Check all 7 sequentially:

| # | Marker | Location | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Healer Sign** (天医线) | 4+ parallel vertical lines below little finger (Mercury mount) | Natural healer; 4–5 = strong potential; 6–7 = destined path; 8+ = past-life master level |
| 2 | **Mystic Cross** (修仙十字) | Clear cross between head and heart lines | Strong intuition; witch nature; typically self-aware from childhood |
| 3 | **Clairvoyance Cross** (灵视十字) | Cross on the middle phalange of index finger | Specifically predictive ability (clairvoyance) — distinct from general witch aptitude |
| 4 | **Psychic Cross(es)** (灵媒十字) | Crosses on mounts below each finger | Jupiter = wisdom master potential; Saturn = meditation/dreamwork; Apollo = manifestation; Mercury = direct magical affinity |
| 5 | **Intuition Circle** (灵感圈) | Small circle at palm base | Predictive ability (similar to Clairvoyance Cross); rarely both present simultaneously |
| 6 | **Astral Travel Lines** (灵游线) | Vertical lines below the Intuition Circle | **Only valid if Intuition Circle is present.** Capacity for altered states and astral projection |
| 7 | **Psychic Triangle** (灵媒三角) | Triangle near palm edge (colour, texture, or line formation) | Strong suppressed/latent ability not yet expressing; a sealed potential |

**Scoring**:
- 5+ markers: Blood-witch material in tradition; extraordinary raw capacity
- 3–4: Clearly above baseline; intuition and sensitivity markedly elevated
- 1–2: Inclination present but not dominant

**Supplementary Cheiro markers** (cross-verify, not part of the 7):
- Luna mount prominent: Imagination and intuition hardware
- Head line deeply descending toward Luna: Intuitive rather than logical processing
- Complete Girdle of Venus: Hypersensitive to emotional atmosphere; picks up others' energy involuntarily
- Intuition line (Luna arc): Systematic, reliable prediction gift

**Tradition note**: Some witch lineages test for these markers before teaching divination skills (tarot, tea leaves). Without them, they consider the teaching wasted. Non-intuitive knowledge (astrology as system) is taught freely; gift-dependent skills are not.

**Sensitivity note**: People with strong markers often feel inexplicably drained in crowded spaces. Over time they may struggle to distinguish their own emotions from ambient absorbed energy.

## Section 9 — Time Reading

### Life Line / Head Line timing
- Origin (thumb-side start) = early childhood
- Mid-line ≈ 35–40 years old
- End = later life

### Fate Line timing
- Where it begins = career/life start point
- Where it crosses head line ≈ age 35
- Where it crosses heart line ≈ age 45–50

### Critical timing rule
**Never default all events to "later in life."** Every break, island, fork, or mark must be dated by its specific position on the line. A fork at the mid-point of the life line means mid-life relocation — not old age. When multiple negative marks align at the same timeline position across different lines, call it explicitly: compounded crisis, not a series of separate small events.

## Section 10 — Reading Style and Voice

1. **Observe the whole before the detail** — hand shape, texture, overall quality first; lines second
2. **Always compare both hands** — always ask which is dominant
3. **State tendencies, not absolutes** — "this line suggests you may..." not "you will..."
4. **Use classical terms but explain them** — "Jupiter mount" is fine followed by a quick gloss
5. **Heart line gets the most questions** — handle with precision and warmth; be thorough
6. **Tell the truth** — negative indicators must be stated. The value of palmistry is honesty. Sugarcoating destroys credibility. Soften delivery, not content.
7. **On hand type challenges** — if the user disputes the hand type call, re-evaluate all four indicators explicitly rather than defending the initial call. Accuracy over consistency.
8. **Multiple images** — if the user provides several photos (back, side, flat, cupped), synthesise all. Side views are essential for union lines, minor edge lines, mount prominence.
9. **End with synthesis** — never leave the user with a disconnected list of features. Integrate: "Taken together, this hand shows a person who..."

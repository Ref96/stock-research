## Source: Nathaniel Altman, *Sexual Palmistry* (1986) + Cheiro integration

## Chapter A — The Five Types of Lover

Based on Altman's psychosexual hand classification. This is a **relational** overlay on the hand-shape system, focused specifically on how a person functions in love and intimacy.

### The Framework: Receptive vs Realistic

All hands fall into two broad meta-categories:
- **Receptive**: Delicate, long, conic in appearance. Owners tend to be emotional, romantic, sensitive. Rich line pattern = complex personality and nervous energy.
- **Realistic**: Broader, more outgoing in appearance. Owners tend toward physicality and three-dimensional engagement with the world.

---

### Type 1: The Square Hand — The Organiser (Realistic)

**Visual**: Squarish palm, squarish fingertips. Often firm or moderately firm consistency.

**Love and sex profile**:
- Common sense and reason dominate over emotion. Evaluates a potential partner practically before committing: stability, reliability, shared goals.
- Once committed, warmer and more loving feelings emerge — but they arrive after trust is established, not before.
- Conventional in sexual habits unless the head line curves toward Luna (adding imagination) or the thumb/hand are flexible (adding adaptability).
- Difficulty adapting to new circumstances — easily falls into sexual routine.
- Positive: Dependable, stable, patient, capable of weathering difficulties in a relationship.
- Challenge: Stiffness in hand = limited flexibility in approach to partners; may demand conformity.

**Ideal partners for Square types**: Someone who shares their structural worldview, or someone who brings warmth without requiring the Square type to abandon their need for predictability.

---

### Type 2: The Spatulate Hand — The Adventurer (Realistic)

**Visual**: Fingertips fan outward like a palette knife. Often strong and broad. Slightly knotted fingers common.

**Love and sex profile**:
- Energetic, self-confident, tenacious. Takes advantage of opportunity. Knows how to flirt.
- Charming, impulsive, exciting to be with — and occasionally infuriating. Will flirt even in an established relationship.
- Unless other parts of the hand (firm thumb, clear head line) moderate the adventurous tendency, maintaining monogamy can be difficult. Potential for simultaneous involvements.
- Highly sexed; sensuality can border on hedonistic.
- When hand is flexible and soft, pursuit of physical pleasure may take precedence over commitments.
- Challenge: Needs ongoing excitement. A relationship that becomes routine will lose this type.

**Ideal partners**: Someone who can provide novelty and match their physical energy, or who is secure enough not to need constant reassurance.

---

### Type 3: The Conic Hand — The Romantic (Receptive)

**Visual**: Slightly tapered palm and fingertips. Skin often fine. Lines typically numerous.

**Love and sex profile**:
- Governed by impulse and first impressions. Falls in love fast and intensely.
- Inconsistency is the main challenge: alternates between peaks of devotion and troughs of withdrawal. Can be intensely devoted one day, distant the next.
- If the hand is firm, commitment is more stable and sustained — the conic type has real follow-through.
- Creative and spontaneous; dislikes routine in bed and in relationship.
- Increases sexual versatility and innovation; thrives on variety of expression.
- When the hand is bland/fat rather than fine/firm, the sensuous nature dominates entirely — food, sex, physical comfort are primary needs.

**Ideal partners**: Someone with enough groundedness to tolerate inconsistency, or someone whose own creativity complements the Conic's spontaneity.

---

### Type 4: The Psychic Hand — The Painful Idealist (Receptive)

**Visual**: Long, graceful fingers with pointed tips. Relatively rare.

**Love and sex profile**:
- Deeply romantic, impressionable, and emotional — more so than the Conic.
- Motivated by profound feeling; often lacks practical reason and common sense in relationships.
- Highly idealistic — frequently out of touch with the day-to-day work needed to sustain a relationship.
- Naive to the point of repeated disappointment.
- Needs grounded, steady, sensitive partners who can help them function in practical reality.
- Cannot thrive with a partner who is also ungrounded — will spin together into unreality.

**Ideal partners**: Earth-element or firm-handed individuals with warmth; someone who offers security without stifling the Psychic's rich inner world.

---

### Type 5: The Mixed Hand — All of the Above

**Visual**: Characteristics of two or more types present. The dominant type provides the foundation; secondary elements modify.

**Love and sex profile**:
- Most people fall here. The primary hand type establishes the base personality; secondary features shift the expression.
- A mostly-square hand with conic fingertips = organiser with unexpected romantic flashes.
- A mostly-conic hand with a long, straight head line = romantic with more practical follow-through than typical.
- For Mixed hands: **always identify the dominant type first, then list the modifiers explicitly.**

---

### Modifying Factors (apply to all five types)

**Hand consistency** (dominant modifier of type expression):

| Consistency | Sexual expression |
|---|---|
| Flabby | Low physical energy; prefers to receive; emotionally flat; may dream more than act |
| Soft + thick | Sensuality heightened; over-indulgence in food, sex, comfort |
| Elastic | Vital, adaptable, open to new sexual experience |
| Firm | Stable, potent; difficulty adapting to partner's new ideas |
| Hard | High energy; psychologically rigid; emotional withholding; anger more easily expressed than tenderness |

**Hand flexibility** (secondary modifier):

| Flexibility | Sexual expression |
|---|---|
| Very flexible | Highly impressionable; generous; potential difficulty with commitment; unpredictable |
| Moderate | Versatile emotionally and sexually; some spread of desire across partners |
| Firm | Careful; reliable; slow to trust but deeply loyal once committed |
| Stiff | Rigid; difficulty compromising; deep feelings present but blocked from expression |

**Hand size** (relative to body):
- Small hands: Intuitive, spontaneous; gravitates toward the dramatic and physically erotic
- Large hands: More attentive; slower and more deliberate in lovemaking; focused on partner's particulars

**Skin texture**:
- Fine: Highly sensitive to environment and to partner; may require harmonious conditions to fully open
- Coarse: More resilient; less attuned to subtle cues; may need explicit direction from partner

**Nail colour** (Altman):
- Reddish: Strong drive; expresses passion in physical terms; may be aggressive
- Pink: Balanced between physical and emotional expression
- Bluish/pale: Drive may be strong internally but takes longer to "warm up"; body doesn't lead

## Chapter B — Sexual Self: The Triad (Altman)

All sexual personality is built on three interlocking forces:

1. **Vital Force** (life energy / prana / ch'i) — the raw fuel
2. **Pleasure Principle** — capacity to give and receive
3. **Aggression** (positive assertion) — the drive to initiate, approach, claim

None of the three can be read from one sign alone. Build the picture across hand shape + Venus mount + life line + heart line + thumb.

### Reading Vital Force
- Primary indicators: **Venus mount size and consistency** + **life line arc**
- Venus mount occupying approx. 1/3 of the palmar surface, bordered by a graceful arc = healthy baseline
- Very large Venus: Potentially overwhelming physical appetite; needs strong head line and thumb as counterbalance; suggest channelling into vigorous physical activity
- Flat + soft Venus: Low vitality; possible difficulty expressing love physically; not necessarily emotionally cold

### Reading the Pleasure Capacity
- Thick, soft, fleshy hand + short smooth fingers + fine pinkish skin = highly sensuous baseline
- Deep, slightly chained heart line (adds sensitivity and emotional depth) enhances this
- Prominent Luna mount (feeds imagination into pleasure)
- Pink nails + flexible hand = heightened responsiveness
- Thin, bony hand + small Venus + cold skin + long knotted fingers + pale nails + weak lines = pleasure is not a priority; body is not the primary vehicle of relating

### Reading Aggression / Initiative
- Firmer consistency = more assertive personality
- Long, firm, low-set thumb = clear sexual self-assertion
- Reddish skin/nails = stronger urgency
- Large Luna mount line of adventure (horizontal marks) = desire for variety and risk
- Large, hard, reddish Venus = energy intensity that can tip toward sexual aggressiveness — cross-check with head line strength

## Chapter C — Personality Traits and Sexual Expression

### Romantic vs Realist

**Romantic indicators**:
- Long, chained or broken heart line (ending at Jupiter or between fingers)
- Head line drooping toward Luna
- Conic or Psychic hand shape
- Apollo finger bending toward Saturn (tendency to idealise partner)
- Large Venus mount + Girdle of Venus

**Realist indicators**:
- Square or Spatulate hand
- Heart line ending at Saturn or just beyond ("physical" type)
- Straight head line
- Mercury finger with slight inward curve (shrewd, astute)

> Note: Many romantics actively perform realism. Look for the mismatch between chained/broken heart line and squarish hand — that person is a romantic wearing practical camouflage.

### Communication Ability

- Long Mercury finger (reaching top phalange of Apollo): Natural communicator; comfortable sharing feelings; found in writers, teachers, public figures
- Short, low-set Mercury: Social discomfort; easily misunderstood; may be shrewder than apparent (Apollo compensates)
- Mercury curving toward Apollo: Charming and seductive; reads situations quickly; may use communication strategically
- Wide space between head and heart lines: Wants to share; open
- Narrow space between lines: Private; secretive; excellent at keeping confidences

### Extroversion / Sexual Inhibition

- Thumb angle when hand opens naturally:
  - 45° (high-set): Victorian sexual attitudes; overly cautious; difficulty letting go; "uptight"
  - 60°: More open than Victorian but still guardedness present
  - 90° (low-set): Self-accepting; sexually integrated; adventurous; comfortable in body
- Life and head lines separate at origin → increases spontaneity and reduces inhibition
- Knotted fingers → increases sexual inhibition (too much analysis)
- Fingers held close together when relaxed → psychological insecurity; guardedness

### Self-Esteem and Jealousy

- Index = Apollo length: Healthy self-esteem
- Index shorter than Apollo: Undervalues self; seeks relationships for self-image repair; more easily manipulated
- Index longer, strong: High self-regard; real confidence — unless life/head joined (then vanity compensating insecurity)
- Index curving toward Saturn: Jealousy proportional to degree of curve
- Early-life trauma: Look at non-dominant hand for baseline; compare to dominant hand change

### Faithfulness Indicators

No certain sign of faithfulness. But these combine:
- Firm to rigid hand + clear heart line ending under Jupiter = enhanced loyalty; reliability not primarily dependent on sexual satisfaction
- Very flexible hand + "generous" thumb + separated head/life lines = more spontaneous; less reliably committed
- Very long, chained heart line = appetite for variety; difficulty with total devotion
- Heart line ending far under Jupiter with bent Apollo = chronic relational dissatisfaction; "shops around"

### Fantasy Profile

- Head line dropping toward Luna + large Luna mount = strong imagination; inner world richer than outer expression suggests
- When this combination includes a flexible hand: Fantasy is available to act on but may remain private
- When combined with stiff hand: Rich fantasy life that remains entirely internal; partner may not know it exists

### Dominant / Submissive Patterns

Classic indicators for dominance:
- Long, strong, low-set thumb
- Strong, long Jupiter finger
- Separated head and life lines at origin
- Clear head line (focus and direction)
- Firm, slightly inflexible hand
- Strong Jupiter and Mars mounts

Classic indicators for submission/passivity:
- Short, weak, high-set thumb
- Weak or short Jupiter
- Soft Mars

**Altman's nuance**: Dominance and submission coexist in most people and shift by relationship context. The aggressive executive may be entirely submissive in intimacy. Read the pattern; ask which role is primary self-expression vs which is compensation for fear.

## Chapter D — Compatibility Analysis

> Load this section when the user provides information about **two** people's hands and asks about their compatibility.

### The Framework

Compatibility is assessed across multiple dimensions. Differences in one or two areas are normal. Differences across **four or more** major dimensions indicate fundamentally mismatched orientation.

### Dimension 1: Hand Shape (Temperament Baseline)

- Square + Conic together: Very different worldviews. Square wants structure and routine; Conic wants spontaneity and feeling.
- Square + Square: High structural harmony; risk of rigid shared ruts.
- Conic + Conic: Passionate but unstable; both need the other to ground them, neither can.
- Spatulate + Psychic: One needs adventure and action; the other needs protective stillness. Potentially explosive.
- Mixed hands: Evaluate by dominant type.

Modifying factors: A square hand with separated head/life lines has more spontaneity. A conic hand with a straight long head line is more practical than typical.

### Dimension 2: Venus Mount (Sexual Energy Match)

**The most important compatibility factor for physical relationships.**

- Both partners should have Venus mounts proportional to their respective body builds.
- One high-drive + one low-drive: Significant long-term strain unless consciously managed.
- Both large and hard Venus: Potential for mutual satisfaction but also power struggle.
- Both soft or flat: Low shared physical priority; relationship survives on intellectual or spiritual compatibility.

### Dimension 3: Heart Line (Emotional Style)

- "Physical" heart line (ending at Saturn or just beyond): Values the purely sexual aspects of love; more direct
- "Mental" heart line (ending at Jupiter or between fingers): Needs emotional support, soft words, cuddling; less focused on physical mechanics
- Physical + Mental pairing: Can work with communication, but may have chronic misunderstanding about what "love" looks like in practice
- Both mental: Deep emotional resonance but possible lack of physical initiative

### Dimension 4: Sensitivity Level

- Ideally roughly matched. Two hypersensitive people (fine skin + long chained heart lines) can trigger each other into escalation; every conflict becomes catastrophic.
- One slightly less sensitive than the other = counterpoint and balance.

### Dimension 5: Flexibility and Consistency

- Similar flexibility = compatible adaptability to life challenges.
- Both stiff: Stubborn standoff when conflict arises; neither compromises. Watch for this in conflict style.
- Both very flexible: Neither can anchor the other; relationship lacks structure.

### Dimension 6: Communication (Mercury finger)

- Long Mercury on both: Highly communicative; may argue more but resolve more.
- Short Mercury on one or both: Risk of silent build-up; misunderstandings harden into resentment.

### Dimension 7: Thumb (Will and Generosity)

- Both stiff: Power struggle; difficulty in mutual accommodation.
- Both generous: May over-give to each other's detriment; neither holds boundaries.
- One generous, one firm: Potentially exploitative if the firm person takes advantage; can also be a healthy complementarity if each respects the other's style.

### Compatibility Reading Protocol

1. Read each person's hand individually first. Never jump to comparison prematurely.
2. Identify each person's hand type, Venus, heart line, head line, thumb, flexibility, skin.
3. Compare dimension by dimension.
4. State where harmony is strong (reinforcement).
5. State where friction is likely (not inevitable failure — these are growth points).
6. Offer reframes for the friction areas: "This difference means you'll need to..."
7. Never advise "stay together" or "leave." That decision belongs to the people. The palmist's role is to make both people more conscious of what they're working with.

**Union lines (婚姻线) as relationship confirmation**:
- Located on Mercury mount above heart line
- Lower = earlier relationship; higher = later
- Long + clear + unbroken = strong relationship with enduring potential
- Islands or breaks = difficulty during that period
- Droop downward at end = subject likely outlives partner
- Split at end = difficult separation or major transformation of relationship nature
- Radical upward or downward curve = difficulty forming deep attachment at all
- Absent = similar difficulty; tiny weak lines = brief superficial involvements

> Union lines are NOT exclusively marriage. They indicate any primary relationship of depth — including long-term close friendship when the legal marriage is emotionally distant.

## Chapter E — Sexual Indicators: Combined Reading Framework

When a user asks specifically about sexual nature, read these indicators together:

| Indicator | What it shows |
|---|---|
| Venus mount size + consistency | Desire baseline and vitality |
| Heart line type (physical vs mental) | Whether sex and emotion are separable for this person |
| Head line descent toward Luna | Strength of sexual fantasy life |
| Girdle of Venus | Sensory hypersensitivity; complete = very high; incomplete = unpredictably on/off |
| Thumb flexibility | Courage to act on or express desire |
| Hand consistency | Energy level and physical expressiveness |
| Nail colour | Speed and intensity of physical response |

### Common Combined Profiles

**High desire, low expression**:
- Large, soft Venus + heart line ending between index/middle fingers + flexible hand with thin consistency
- Wants deeply but ties sex to emotional safety; without emotional foundation, cannot fully engage physically

**Rich fantasy, low action**:
- Head line strongly descending toward Luna + large Luna + soft thumb
- Inner world far bolder than outer presentation. Likely has thoughts/desires never shared with any partner.

**Hot-cold pattern**:
- Incomplete Girdle of Venus + forked heart line
- Highly engaged when conditions align; completely shut down when they don't. Partners find this bewildering.

**Post-wound closure**:
- Heart line with downward midpoint fork + large active Venus
- Not lack of desire — desire is fully present. But the wound built a wall. Physical intimacy = emotional vulnerability; reopening = trusting again. This is the core tension.
